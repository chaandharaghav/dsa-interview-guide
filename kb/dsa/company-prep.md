# Company-Specific Interview Preparation

Each company has its own interview style and focus areas. Here's how to tailor your prep.

---

## 🔵 Google

### Interview Style
- **Rounds:** 4-5 coding interviews (45 min each)
- **Focus:** Algorithmic problem-solving, code quality, communication
- **Difficulty:** Often starts medium, goes to hard
- **Unique:** May ask follow-up variations; values optimization discussion

### High-Priority Patterns
1. **Dynamic Programming** - Google LOVES DP
2. **Graph Algorithms** - BFS/DFS, shortest paths
3. **String Manipulation** - Substrings, pattern matching
4. **Binary Search Variations** - Especially on answer space

### Frequently Asked Topics
| Topic | Frequency | Example Problems |
|-------|-----------|------------------|
| DP | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Word Break, Coin Change, LIS |
| Graphs | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Number of Islands, Word Ladder |
| Arrays/Strings | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Longest Substring, Median of Arrays |
| Trees | ⭐⭐⭐ | LCA, Serialize/Deserialize |
| Design | ⭐⭐⭐ | LRU Cache, Min Stack |

### Google-Specific Problems to Focus On
- [ ] LC 4: Median of Two Sorted Arrays (Hard) ⭐⭐
- [ ] LC 42: Trapping Rain Water (Hard) ⭐⭐
- [ ] LC 76: Minimum Window Substring (Hard) ⭐⭐
- [ ] LC 127: Word Ladder (Hard) ⭐
- [ ] LC 295: Find Median from Data Stream (Hard) ⭐
- [ ] LC 329: Longest Increasing Path in a Matrix (Hard) ⭐
- [ ] LC 403: Frog Jump (Hard) ⭐
- [ ] LC 410: Split Array Largest Sum (Hard) ⭐
- [ ] LC 855: Exam Room (Medium) ⭐
- [ ] LC 843: Guess the Word (Hard) - Interactive!

### Google Interview Tips
1. **Think out loud constantly** - They want to see your thought process
2. **Start with brute force** - Then optimize step by step
3. **Ask about edge cases** - Shows thoroughness
4. **Discuss trade-offs** - Time vs space, different approaches
5. **Write clean code** - They care about code quality

## 🟠 Amazon

### Interview Style
- **Rounds:** 4-5 interviews (combination of coding + Leadership Principles)
- **Focus:** Practical problem-solving, scalability thinking, LP stories
- **Difficulty:** Medium-focused, occasionally hard
- **Unique:** Heavy emphasis on Leadership Principles (LP)

### High-Priority Patterns
1. **Arrays & Strings** - Very common
2. **Trees & Graphs** - BFS especially
3. **Design Questions** - OOP design, simple system design
4. **Greedy** - Scheduling, optimization

### Leadership Principles to Prepare (Technical Context)
- **Customer Obsession:** "How would this solution impact user experience?"
- **Ownership:** "I took responsibility for..."
- **Dive Deep:** Technical deep-dives into your past projects
- **Bias for Action:** "I made this decision quickly because..."
- **Deliver Results:** Metrics, impact of your work

### Frequently Asked Topics
| Topic | Frequency | Example Problems |
|-------|-----------|------------------|
| Arrays | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Two Sum, Product of Array |
| Trees | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Validate BST, Right Side View |
| Strings | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Group Anagrams, Longest Palindrome |
| Graphs | ⭐⭐⭐ | Number of Islands, Course Schedule |
| OOP Design | ⭐⭐⭐ | Parking Lot, Library System |

### Amazon-Specific Problems to Focus On

- [ ] LC 937: Reorder Data in Log Files (Medium) ⭐
- [ ] LC 973: K Closest Points to Origin (Medium) ⭐

### Amazon Interview Tips
1. **Prepare LP stories** - Have 6-8 stories covering multiple LPs
2. **Use the STAR method** - Situation, Task, Action, Result
3. **Mention scale** - Amazon loves hearing about handling large data
4. **Be practical** - Working solution > perfect solution
5. **Test your code** - They want to see you catch bugs

## 🔷 Meta (Facebook)

### Interview Style
- **Rounds:** 2 coding (45 min each) + 1 system design (for senior) + 1 behavioral
- **Focus:** Speed and accuracy, optimal solutions
- **Difficulty:** Medium to Hard
- **Unique:** Expect 2 problems per 45-minute round!

### High-Priority Patterns
1. **Arrays & Strings** - Very heavy
2. **Binary Trees** - Especially traversals
3. **Graphs** - BFS-heavy problems
4. **Intervals** - Merging, scheduling

### Frequently Asked Topics

|-------|-----------|------------------|

### Meta Interview Tips
1. **Be FAST** - 2 problems in 45 minutes means ~20 min each
2. **Practice writing code quickly** - No time to think too long
3. **Clarify quickly** - Don't spend 5 minutes asking questions
4. **Have templates memorized** - BFS, DFS, etc.
5. **Optimize as you go** - Don't write brute force then rewrite

## 📅 2-Week Company-Specific Sprint

If you have 2 weeks before a specific company interview:

### Week 1: Core Problems
- Days 1-2: Company's top 10 most-asked problems
- Days 3-4: Pattern-specific problems (based on company focus)
- Days 5-6: Medium difficulty practice
- Day 7: Review, identify weak areas

### Week 2: Polish & Practice
- Days 1-2: Hard problems from company's list
- Days 3-4: Mock interviews (timed)
- Day 5: Behavioral prep (for Amazon: LP stories)
- Day 6: Light review, rest
- Day 7: Interview day!
